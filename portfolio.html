Weddings

Full ceremonies, pre-wedding sessions, and the quiet moments in between.

01 — Traditional Rites

Every detail earns its close-up

Traditional ceremonies move fast and carry a lot of meaning in small details — the beadwork, the regalia, the way a couple holds themselves once they're officially presented. Those are the frames I stay ready for.

02 — The Second Outfit

Five minutes before the reception

A lot of brides change outfits at least once, and the second look always feels a little like a secret. Gold light, a quiet room, and a few minutes before it fills back up with guests.

03 — White On White

Bridal, without the wedding day pressure

Not every white-dress moment has to happen on the actual wedding day. This one was pure pre-wedding indulgence — pearls stacked high, a dress built for drama, and none of the timeline stress a real ceremony comes with.

04 — Held Close

The embrace before the reception

Shot at GOLifestyle House, in the minute right after the formal photos wrapped and they just held onto each other instead. Some of the best wedding images happen the second no one's posing anymore.

Portraits & Milestones

Maternity, graduations, birthdays, and family sessions — the moments people mark on purpose.

01 — Sisters

A pose built on purpose

These two are sisters, not strangers to the camera — and that pose was fully planned, right down to the framing. Sometimes the strongest shot comes from a clear idea, not a happy accident.

02 — New Life

Waiting on a whole new life

This wasn't just a maternity shoot — it was a portrait of a life about to change completely. Bare feet finding balance on wet stone, hands raised like she's already holding what's coming. Some sessions are about a look; this one was about a beginning.

03 — Birthday

A birthday, properly celebrated

Birthdays deserve more than a quick phone snap of the cake. This session was about the whole feeling of the day — the candles, her name iced across the cake, the joy of being celebrated on purpose. Small joys deserve the same care as the big ones.

04 — Conferred

Making the years show

A graduation portrait has one job: make the years show. The gown does the talking — I just find the light that makes the tassel catch.

05 — Four, Outdoors

Waiting out the toddler and the wind

Family sessions live or die on patience — waiting out the toddler, the wind, the one good smile from everyone at once. Worth every extra frame it takes to get there.

06 — Henna & Lace

A little detail worth its own moment

Fresh henna deserves better than a quick phone photo before it fades. This session was just about the detail itself — mint fabric, gold jewelry, and hands worth showing off.

Editorial

Concept-driven shoots, bold styling, and fashion-magazine energy.

01 — Studio Heat

A colour that refuses to be ignored

Sometimes a shoot doesn't need a concept — just a print that won't be ignored and a subject who already knows what to do with her chin.

02 — Ignition

The night he won

Smoke, traffic lights, and a look sharp enough to take first place at a fashion show. When a client brings a concept this strong, I bring the lighting to match it.

03 — Wild Green

Editorial doesn't need a studio

Not every backdrop needs four walls. Overgrown leaves, hard midday sun, and a red lip that refuses to fade into the shade.

04 — After Dark

Old Hollywood, new plate number

A convertible, a silk headscarf, and just enough streetlight to work with. Some editorial ideas don't need a studio — this one just needed a driveway after sunset.

Brand

Business portraits and brand event coverage for people who need their images to work as hard as they do.

01 — Brand, Not Just Business

A portrait that works as hard as she does

Small business owners need portraits that work as hard as they do — on a website, a menu, a business card. This one's for someone who takes her craft as seriously as I take mine.

02 — Launch Night

Covering a brand's big moment

A brand launch is its own kind of event coverage — new collection styled on the mannequins, press and guests moving through the space, the founder finally exhaling long enough for a photo. I shoot these to capture the atmosphere and the product with equal attention, not just a room full of smiling faces.

03 — AA Clothiers x Odumodu Blvck

When the name in the room does the talking

Some brand shoots get a bigger boost than others — this one had Odumodu Blvck himself sitting in frame for AA Clothiers, effortless as ever. When a face that recognizable is in the room, the job is simple: find the light, get the angle, and let him do the rest.

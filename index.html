I photograph the days people spend months planning for — and the small ones nobody plans for at all.

Under the name camerababe, I shoot weddings, traditional ceremonies, maternity sessions, milestones and brand portraits with one instinct running through all of it: light like editorial, feeling like documentary. Every gallery on this page is a real shoot — engagements, bridal work, birthdays, family sessions, business portraits, and the occasional fashion story shot because the light was too good to waste.

If you want photographs that still hold up once the excitement of the day has worn off, that's the work I do.

"Light like editorial, feeling like documentary."
01 — Portfolio

The stories

Fourteen frames, fourteen reasons someone called a photographer.

01 — Unscripted

The pose nobody planned

Two friends, one backdrop, and a pose that only worked because nobody overthought it. The best portraits are usually the ones where the laughter sneaks into the frame.

02 — Traditional Rites

Every detail earns its close-up

Traditional ceremonies move fast and carry a lot of meaning in small details — the beadwork, the regalia, the way a couple holds themselves once they're officially presented. Those are the frames I stay ready for.

03 — Studio Heat

A colour that refuses to be ignored

Sometimes a shoot doesn't need a concept — just a print that won't be ignored and a subject who already knows what to do with her chin.

04 — The Second Outfit

Five minutes before the reception

A lot of brides change outfits at least once, and the second look always feels a little like a secret. Gold light, a quiet room, and a few minutes before it fills back up with guests.

05 — Waiting

A body finding its own balance

Maternity sessions ask for patience more than posing — bare feet finding balance on wet stone, hands raised like she's about to catch something.

06 — Candles & Cake

Small joys, same good light

Not every shoot is a wedding. Some are a cake, a candle, and someone's name written in icing — small joys deserve the same care as the big ones.

07 — Ignition

A bold idea taken seriously

This one started as a birthday request and turned into a full production — smoke, traffic lights, a borrowed bike. Bring me a bold idea and I'll bring the lighting to match it.

08 — Conferred

Making the years show

A graduation portrait has one job: make the years show. The gown does the talking — I just find the light that makes the tassel catch.

09 — The Announcement

The second the news lands

Celebration parties like this one move fast — balloons deflate, cake gets cut, the room empties. I stay close enough to catch the exact moment the news lands on someone's face.

10 — Fitting Room

Selling the dress before the try-on

Bridal campaigns need a gown that photographs as well as it fits. An archway, real florals, and a dress built like architecture — the kind of shoot that sells it before anyone tries it on.

11 — After, Before

The quiet minute after "I do"

The quiet minute after the ceremony, before the reception noise starts — just two people, a bouquet, and a staircase that finally isn't in the way.

12 — Four, Outdoors

Waiting out the toddler and the wind

Family sessions live or die on patience — waiting out the toddler, the wind, the one good smile from everyone at once. Worth every extra frame it takes to get there.

13 — Brand, Not Just Business

A portrait that works as hard as she does

Small business owners need portraits that work as hard as they do — on a website, a menu, a business card. This one's for someone who takes her craft as seriously as I take mine.

14 — Wild Green

Editorial doesn't need a studio

Not every backdrop needs four walls. Overgrown leaves, hard midday sun, and a red lip that refuses to fade into the shade.

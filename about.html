About

The eye behind camerababe

Editorial instincts, documentary honesty — a photographer working across weddings, lifestyle, and brand imagery.

I photograph the days people spend months planning for — and the small ones nobody plans for at all.

Under the name camerababe, I shoot weddings, lifestyle, maternity sessions, milestones and brand portraits with one instinct running through all of it: light like editorial, feeling like documentary. Every gallery on this site is a real shoot — engagements, bridal work, birthdays, family sessions, business portraits, and the occasional fashion story shot because the light was too good to waste.

If you want photographs that still hold up once the excitement of the day has worn off, that's the work I do.

02 — FAQ

Good to know

How far in advance should I book?

Book ahead of time where you can — same-day bookings are accepted but attract an additional fee.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — a 50% deposit secures your session, or 70% for weddings. The remaining balance is due on the day of the shoot.

How long until I get my photos?

Photos are delivered within 74 hours. Turnaround for event coverage depends on the size of the event. Videos take 5 days, excluding event videography.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. Shoots outside the city centre carry an additional travel fee, and out-of-state travel needs to be reserved in advance.

Policy

Booking policies

Please read these before making payment — they cover how bookings, deposits, rescheduling, and delivery work.

01 — Location & Travel

Where a shoot happens

Do you have a studio?

No — I don't own a studio. You choose the location, and recommendations can be provided if you're not sure where to shoot.

Do you travel outside Abuja?

Yes. I'm based in Abuja and available to travel — out-of-town shoots attract additional fees.

Shooting at a restaurant, bar, or venue?

You're responsible for complying with that venue's minimum spend policy. Noncompliance may lead to cancellation of the shoot, with a 50% refund.

02 — Booking & Payments

Securing your date

How much deposit do I need to pay?

A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance must be paid immediately after the session or event.

Is my deposit refundable?

No. Deposits are non-refundable in the case of an appointment cancellation.

How do I pay my deposit?

Pay securely online by card, bank transfer, or USSD from the Booking page, or send a direct bank transfer — both options are available once you're quoted.

03 — Session Duration & Extra Charges

What's included, and what isn't

How long does a session last?

Sessions last 1 hour. Your time starts counting from your scheduled appointment — late arrivals will have their session adjusted to fit the remaining time.

Can I extend my session?

Yes, subject to availability — extra time is ₦15,000 per 30 minutes.

Are there charges for same-day or late-evening bookings?

Same-day bookings incur an additional ₦10,000 charge. Shoots from 7PM upward also attract an extra ₦10,000 charge.

04 — Cancellation & Rescheduling

Change of plans

What happens if I don't show up?

Failure to show up within the arranged time results in automatic cancellation, with no refund.

How do I reschedule?

Rescheduling must be done at least 24 hours before your shoot. Same-day rescheduling attracts an additional ₦10,000 fee.

05 — Editing & Delivery

Getting your images and video

How long does editing take?

Photo editing takes 3 days, and video editing takes 5 days, after your selects are chosen.

Can I get my photos faster?

Yes — express editing is available at an extra fee.

Can you edit photos you didn't take?

Yes, I offer editing for pictures I didn't personally shoot.

06 — Other Important Notes

A few more things to know

What equipment do you shoot on?

A digital camera and a mobile phone, depending on the shoot.

Are your rates negotiable?

My rates are fixed.

Do you work on Sundays?

No, I don't work on Sundays.

Can my images be used for promotional purposes?

By booking, you grant permission for your images to be used for promotional purposes.

Kindly review these policies before making payment. Thank you.
